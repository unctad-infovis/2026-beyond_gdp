### Why go beyond GDP?

Gross domestic product (GDP) has long been the leading indicator used to measure progress and assess countries' performance.

GDP measures economic output. But it doesn't show whether people are living well, whether production and consumption are harming the planet, whether opportunities are shared or whether progress can continue over time.

These limitations are reflected in real-world outcomes. GDP growth has frequently been accompanied by persistent inequality, ecological degradation and declining public trust in institutions.

Episodic shocks, such as financial crises and the COVID-19 pandemic, have revealed systemic weaknesses. Long-term structural pressures, including environmental degradation, inequality, geopolitical tensions and democratic challenges, have further underscored the limitations of relying on GDP alone.

### What does going beyond GDP mean?

It requires a new understanding of what we mean by progress.

Progress builds on the foundational principles of peace, human rights and respect for the planet, recognizing that:
